; Spaceship zen mode map.
;
; The map is a tall column of areas. Fly to the top or bottom of the screen and keep
; going to scroll into the next one; its name fades in as you arrive. Every area loops
; through its own scenery, so there is always something passing by wherever you are.
;
; AREA <from> <to> <name>
;   from, to  the view positions this area covers, in rows (one screen is 24 rows).
;             Negative is up. You start at 0.
;
; SCENE <gap> <sprite> <depth> <color> <row>      (belongs to the AREA above it)
;   gap     seconds (at cruising speed) after the area's previous scene
;   sprite  any sprite from sprites.txt
;   depth   0 to 1: how close it is. Far things (0.1) crawl past and barely shift
;           when you climb or dive, so they can be seen from neighbouring areas too.
;           Near things (0.8) rush by and slide past quickly.
;   color   0-15, used wherever the sprite has no color of its own
;   row     where it shows on screen while the view is inside its area (2 top, 25 bottom)
